## Contributed notes (version 1.0.2)

- fNIRS recordings of 14 participants receiving electrical pain stimulation (VAS 3/7) before and after morphine or placebo (Peng et al. 2018, Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 12:394, doi:10.3389/fnhum.2018.00394). 105 recordings are labelled task-Electrical and 8 task-TactilePerception; TaskName in all 113 nirs.json files is TactilePerception (published values, unchanged).
- Time unit: the SNIRF files of all 113 recordings declare the time unit "unknown". Read as seconds, their time axes give a sampling rate of 25 Hz, equal to SamplingFrequency in every nirs.json; nothing points to milliseconds.
